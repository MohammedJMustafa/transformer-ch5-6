# Transformer: Chapters 5 and 6 as one lecture

**Open it: https://mohammedjmustafa.github.io/transformer-ch5-6/**

The Homework 2 lecture "Transformer": Chapters 5 and 6 of *Artificial Intelligence for Everyone* summarised as one
lecture, with the equations and diagrams of Chapter 12 of Bishop & Bishop, *Deep Learning: Foundations and Concepts*
(Springer, 2024). Seventeen lessons in four parts, the three key equations (the dot product, softmax and
self-attention) as working tools, nine labs, and a 40-question final check.

The whole page is one file, `index.html`, with its text, figures, fonts and maths built in. Download it and it also
runs offline by double-click.

Mohammed Jalal Mustafa, Erbil Technical Engineering College, Information Systems Engineering Department.
